# Channels

| Channel | Good for | Cadence | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email (own domain) | the second touch, the proposal, the receipt | 2 touches per sequence | G3 |
| LinkedIn (own profile) | the observation, the public comment, the warm open | 1 post/day max, 5 comments/day max | G3 |
| The person's network (intros) | the highest-converting open there is | as offered, never faked | G3 |
| The site + answer surface | inbound that arrives already convinced | weekly page, monthly audit | G1 |
| Communities (forums, Slacks) | listening, and answering where asked | read daily, answer when useful | G3 |

## Rules

- One channel per touch inside a sequence. Two channels in one day reads as a campaign.
- Never touch the same person twice in a week across channels.
- A channel we cannot measure is a channel we do not scale.
- No purchased lists, no scraped personal data, no "automation" that logs in as a human.
