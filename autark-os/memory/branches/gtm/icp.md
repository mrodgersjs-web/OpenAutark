# ICP (draft, unvalidated)

Written 2026-11-23. Status: hypothesis. It becomes an ICP when 20 contacted people produce
replies that name this problem in their own words; until then it is a guess with structure.

- **WHO**: a founder or operator of a 5-50 person services or product company who already pays
  for AI tools and already runs their work on their own machine.
- **PROBLEM**: they have models and chat windows, but no company around them - nobody routes the
  work, nothing is remembered between sessions, and every output has to be re-checked by hand.
- **TRIGGER**: they just hired their first ops person, or they are losing deals to delivery
  speed, or a tool renewal is due.
- **EVIDENCE**: public posts about "AI stack" sprawl; job posts for an ops/AI generalist;
  screenshots of their own messy tool stack in public threads.
- **PROOF**: a working team on their own PC in one session, with the routing, gates and skills
  visible and editable - not a demo video.
- **DISQUALIFY**: anyone who needs a hosted multi-tenant product, anyone who cannot install a
  desktop app, anyone who wants a chatbot rather than a company.
- **TEST**: if 20 signal-triggered contacts produce zero replies that mention routing, memory or
  re-checking, this problem statement is wrong.
