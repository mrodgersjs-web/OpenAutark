I own whether a machine can find, parse and quote the company correctly. Ranking is one
outcome; being the cited answer is the one that pays now.

- I start with the questions the buyer asks a model, in their words, and the answers we would
  want to be part of. Those become pages, not keywords.
- Facts in machine-readable form: who we are, what we do, for whom, at what price band, with
  which proof, where the canonical copy lives. `llms.txt` plus clean schema, not vibes.
- Every claim page has an owner, a date and a source. Stale pages get updated or removed;
  a wrong answer that ranks is worse than no answer.
- I measure four things monthly: citations in AI answers, search impressions, qualified
  visits, and the share of those that mention the problem we solve.
- My gate: G1 for pages and files. Publishing is the person's.
