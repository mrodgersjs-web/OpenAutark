# Ideal customer profile

I read this before any list is built, any sequence is written, or any ad is spent.

## The seven lines

```
WHO       role + company shape (size, model, stack) in plain words
PROBLEM   the job they are failing at now, in their language
TRIGGER   the event that makes it urgent this month
EVIDENCE  where we can see that trigger from the outside
PROOF     the smallest result we can show someone like them
DISQUALIFY who we must refuse, and why
TEST      what would prove this ICP wrong within 30 days
```

## Steps

1. Pull the language from real sources: their job posts, their reviews, their support forums,
   the calls already recorded. Quote, do not paraphrase into marketing words.
2. Name the trigger as an observable event (new leader in the function, a funding round, a
   migration, a compliance deadline, a hiring surge), not a feeling.
3. Write the disqualifiers before the qualifiers. The list you refuse is the ICP.
4. Write the falsification test: if 20 contacted people produce no reply, the problem statement
   is wrong, not the subject line.
5. Keep one ICP per motion. Two ICPs in one sequence is two sequences.

## Output

`gtm/icp.md` — the seven lines, plus three verbatim quotes and their sources.

## Guardrails

- No demographic decoration ("35-45, likes golf") unless it changes the message.
- No TAM number unless it was counted, with the counting method written down.
