# What great looks like

A benchmark of the category Autark competes in, captured rather than remembered. Every claim here
sits next to a screenshot taken on **2026-11-23** at a 1440x900 viewport.

## Method

1. **The set**: Autark itself, three products that sell the same promise (an AI team that does work
   for you), and six that set expectations for the pieces (agent desktop, memory, workflows,
   coding agents, model front ends).
2. **Fix the dimensions before looking**, so the scoring is not reverse-engineered from the shots:
   first impression, one clear action, proof, copy specificity, product visible, design craft,
   motion and feedback, states and empty cases, speed feel, trust and safety surface, price
   clarity, "would I forward this".
3. **Capture**: viewport screenshot, live URL, date, and the headline quoted verbatim.
4. **Score 1-5** with the observation that decided each score. `·` means the capture did not show
   it - not observed, not a zero.
5. **Turn the gaps into moves**: one concrete change each, with effort and expected effect
   (`../../UPGRADES.md`).

## Honest limits

- One viewport per site, desktop only. A product can be excellent below the fold or on a phone.
- Scores are a judgement from the capture, not a measurement. They are useful for spotting
  patterns and useless as a rating of the companies.
- No pricing pages were opened; price clarity is therefore mostly "not observed" by design.
