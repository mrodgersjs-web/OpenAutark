# Offer ladder (draft, no prices set)

Written 2026-11-23. Prices are deliberately blank: the person sets them (G4). Each band names
what it includes and, more importantly, what it refuses.

| Band | Who it is for | Includes | Refuses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup | a team that wants the OS installed and configured once | install, team configured to their work, their first three skills, a handover session | running their work for them |
| Operate | a team already running it who wants the engine tuned | weekly review, skill changes, pipeline and content support, a monthly proof packet | owning their outcomes |
| Build | a team that needs a capability that does not exist yet | a new agent or skill, built with its own check, installed | open-ended development |

**Risk reversal we can honour**: if the installed team cannot produce the first three artifacts
we agreed, we fix it in the same engagement or the setup fee is returned. No promise about their
revenue, ever.

**Excluded from all bands**: outbound sending on their behalf (their accounts, their approvals),
legal or financial advice, anything that requires a credential we should not hold.
